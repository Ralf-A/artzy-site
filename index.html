The car is the client.

Most of this work happens at dusk and after dark, when the paint stops fighting the sky and the city does the lighting. A W140 in front of a floodlit church says more than a W140 on a white backdrop. Rolling shots are panned from a chase car, never faked in Photoshop.

Owners get a set they'll actually print. Dealers get consistent, listing-ready frames. Brands get whatever the brief says, on time.
